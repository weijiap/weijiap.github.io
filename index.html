About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Business Analytics at Sacred Heart University and an applied scientist with 10+ years of experience developing machine learning, forecasting, causal inference, and natural language processing solutions for large-scale structured, unstructured, and time-series data. I enjoy discovering meaningful patterns in complex data and turning them into insights that drive better decisions.
