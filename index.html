About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Business Analytics at the Jack Welch College of Business and Technology, Sacred Heart University.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Rutgers University–New Brunswick in 2020 under the supervision of Professor Norman R. Swanson. My dissertation focused on forecasting methods in high-frequency financial econometrics. Prior to that, I earned a B.A. in Economics and a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Central University of Finance and Economics, China, in 2014.

My research interests include time series analysis and forecasting, machine learning and big data, and FinTech.
